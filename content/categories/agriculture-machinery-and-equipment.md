## Types of Agricultural Machinery and Equipment

### Tractors
Tractors are essential agricultural machinery & equipment used for ploughing, tilling, and transport. They are powerful farm equipment that support multiple farming tasks.

### Harvesters
Harvesters are advanced automated farming equipment used to cut and collect crops quickly. This type of farm equipment saves time and reduces manual labour.

### Rotavators
Rotavators are small agriculture equipment used to prepare soil before planting. These agricultural machinery & equipment improve soil quality and support better crop growth.

### Seed Drills
Seed drills are a good piece of organic farming equipment that lay the seeds down evenly in the soil. This way the fertilization can be adjusted properly and the production improved by using efficient farm machinery and power.

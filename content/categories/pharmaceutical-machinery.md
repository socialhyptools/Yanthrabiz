## Types of Pharmaceutical Machinery

### Tablet Making Machines
Tablet making machines are widely used pharmaceutical machinery that press powder into tablets. These machines are supplied by top pharmaceutical machinery manufacturers for consistent output.

Capsule filling machines are efficient pharma machinery used to fill capsules with powder or liquid. Many pharma machinery manufacturers offer advanced systems for high-speed production.
